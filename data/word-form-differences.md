# 商品原名中的不同词形

同一类别内按首次选中的商品形式保留一张词卡，不因单复数或 Yogurt/Yoghurt 拼写差异拆卡。下表仅列快照里同时出现两种形式的例子。

| 分类 | 形式一及商品例子 | 形式二及商品例子 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| dairy-eggs-fridge | Yogurt：Farmers Union No Added Sugar Kids Yogurt Pouch Strawberry | Yoghurt：Tamar Valley Dairy The Creamery Yoghurt Pouch Mango & Cream |
| bakery | Cookie：Coles Bakery Indulgent White 40% Choc Chip Cookie | Cookies：Coles Oat & Raisin Cookies |
| bakery | Scroll：Coles Cheesy Vegemite Scroll | Scrolls：Coles Bakery Mini Wholemeal Cheesy Vegemite Scrolls |
| international-foods | Dumpling：Samyang Buldak Spicy Dumpling Carbonara | Dumplings：KB's Prawn Hargow Dumplings |
| health-dietary | Yogurt：Farmers Union No Added Sugar Kids Yogurt Pouch Strawberry | Yoghurt：Jalna Farm To Pot Organic Yoghurt Greek Style |
| frozen | Croquette：Coles Finest Triple Smoked Ham & 3 Cheese Croquette | Croquettes：Coles Finest Truffled Potato Croquettes |
| pet | Dog Treat：The Paw Grocer Freeze Dried Salmon Belly With Fins Dog Treat | Dog Treats：Pedigree Dentastix Large Dog Treats Daily Oral Care Dental Chews |

已有水果的 Apple/Apples、Avocado/Avocados、Banana/Bananas、Lemon/Lemons、Orange/Oranges、Pear/Pears 继续合并，保留原词库复数形式。

不同类别可出现相同英文而中文不同，例如零食类 Potato Chips（薯片）与冷冻类 Potato Chips（薯条）；未跨类别强行合并。
